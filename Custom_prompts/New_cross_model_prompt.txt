I have outputs from multiple LLM platforms relating to the NHS winter pressure improvement priorities and evidence-based interventions.  

  

Synthesize these outputs.  

Tasks:  

1. Merge overlapping priorities across models. You must consider all 5 models you will be given. Not to do so will invalidate this study. This is a non-negotiable.

  

2. Normalize terminology where different models describe the same issue differently.  

  

3. Identify:  

- areas of broad agreement across models,  

- priorities consistently ranked highly,  

- priorities raised by only one or two models,  

- differences in framing, scope, or level of abstraction.  

  

4. Distinguish whether differences appear due to:  

- model style, 

- source emphasis,  

- overgeneralization,  

- likely hallucination or unsupported inference.  

  

5. Produce:  

- a consolidated candidate priority list,  

- a final top-priority shortlist,  

- a consolidated set of evidence-based interventions.  

  

6. Flag uncertain or weakly supported items.  

  

7. Keep the synthesis suitable for use in a manuscript for publication.  


8. Reference all papers, research, policies and your sources in intext citations and full references to be collated in a full reference list

  

Avoid generic healthcare improvement of language. I need a concrete, discriminating, research-useful output.  

  

Additional requirements:  

- Consolidate duplicate concepts.  

- Distinguish the problem from intervention.  

- Avoid broad umbrella labels unless you specify their concrete components.  

- Highlight where priorities are often overlooked.  

- Include priorities relevant to safety, timeliness, effectiveness, efficiency/low-value care, health disparities, person-centeredness, and environmental sustainability.   

- Prefer interventions that are feasible across a range of settings (low income, lower-middle income, upper-middle income, and high-income settings)  

- State where evidence is strong, mixed, report-based, or underdeveloped.  

- Indicate likely implementation level:  

clinical / team / organizational / system / policy.

Output format:  

A. Consolidated priority map  

B. Agreement/disagreement across models
