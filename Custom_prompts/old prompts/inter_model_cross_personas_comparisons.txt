Compare the healthcare improvement priorities identified by the following five personas:
1. frontline clinician
2. healthcare improvement practitioner
3. health system leader
4. patient and family caregiver
5. health system steward

Tasks:
1. Identify areas of strong agreement across personas.
2. Identify priorities emphasized by one persona but not others.
3. Explain how stakeholder perspective changes the ranking of priorities.
4. Distinguish:
- priorities likely to be universally selected,
- priorities more operationally focused,
- priorities more strategic/system-level,
- priorities more policy-driven.

5. Identify which priorities appear robust across perspectives and therefore are strongest candidates for an internationally relevant healthcare improvement agenda.

6. Summarize tensions among perspectives, such as:
- frontline practicality vs policy ambition
- feasibility vs equity
- near-term measurability vs long-term transformation
- cost containment vs person-centeredness
- environmental sustainability vs operational constraints

7. Recommend a final balanced list of priorities that integrates all five perspectives.

Output format:
A. Areas of convergence
B. Areas of divergence
C. Perspective-specific blind spots
D. Integrated final priority shortlist
E. Implications for study interpretation
