For each top 10 priority, identify 3 to 7 evidence-based actionable interventions or recommendations.

Instructions:
1. Focus on interventions that are broadly relevant across countries and healthcare systems, while recognizing the need for adaptation to context.

2. Use peer-reviewed evidence and credible domain-specific reports if available.

For each intervention, specify:
- implementation level: whether the intervention is more appropriate at the clinical, organizational, or policy level 
- the mechanism by which the intervention improves care
- amount of resources required (low, medium, high)
- implementation requirements
- strength/type of supporting evidence
- feasibility considerations
- facilitators
- barriers
- scalability/transferability across settings
- suggested indicators or metrics to track progress

4. Distinguish:
- interventions with strong international evidence 
- interventions based mainly on case examples 
- interventions that are promising but under-evaluated.

5. Flag if a recommendation is highly context-sensitive or may be difficult to implement in resource-constrained settings.

6. Avoid vague recommendations such as “improve coordination” unless you specify what concrete actions that means.

7. Do not invent evidence or overstate certainty.

Output format:
A. Priority-by-priority evidence-based intervention list
B. Cross-cutting intervention strategies that apply to multiple priorities
C. Most high-yield high-impact interventions
D. Interventions feasible across a range of settings (low income, lower-middle income, upper-middle income, and high-income settings)
E. Evidence gaps and research needs
